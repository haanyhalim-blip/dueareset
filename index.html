Due a reset?

Most of us run on habits we never chose. Every so often it's worth picking one and drawing a line – stop something, start something, or go without for a few days just to see what happens. It doesn't have to be big. It has to be on purpose. That's a reset.

Say what you're resetting and why, count the days, write down what you notice. Nobody sees your page but you, unless you want them to. And the list below shows what everyone else is resetting right now, so you know you're not the only one.

Start one

People use it for quitting smoking or vaping, cutting sugar, less screen time, a fast or drinking less – or anything else they want to change.

What are you resetting?

Leave "Ends" blank to count up. Give it a date to count down – a fast or a dry month has an end, a quit doesn't.

If you have a health condition or take medication, check with your GP before a fast.
