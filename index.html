From the book The Comfort Crisis

Why an adventure?

In The Comfort Crisis (2021), the journalist Michael Easter argues that modern life has quietly taken the hard parts out of our days. We're rarely cold, hungry, bored or pushed anywhere near our limit, and he thinks we're worse off for it.

His answer is the misogi, a name borrowed from a Japanese purification ritual: a big challenge, taken on on purpose, hard enough that you might not manage it. It has just two rules – make it really hard, and don't die.

The point isn't the challenge itself. It's finding out you can do more than you thought, and bringing that back into the rest of your life. Easter suggests one a year. Even one every few years can reset how you see yourself.
